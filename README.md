LAMPION — a paper lantern's flight over the night market: guide a paper lantern through **seven nights** to the moon. Each night has its own goal — kindle every paper lamp to open the glowing gate, gather the night's wishes, and fly through. Between nights, pick one blessing (bigger flame, extra gust, wish magnet, paper shield, and more). Night 2 bends your light through mirror shards, Night 3 rides river wind bands and paper boats, Night 4 chains kite near-misses, Night 5 burns through a firework storm, Night 6 is a boss fight against the Kite King (kindle his three crown-lamps), and Night 7 is a vertical climb to the moon itself.

A vanilla JS canvas arcade game (mouse + keyboard, flame meter, 3 lives for the whole run, combo scoring, WebAudio synth), no libraries, no network, no external assets.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.

## How to play

- **Move:** follow your mouse, or steer with Arrow keys / WASD.
- **Gust:** Space or click (limited charges, they come back).
- **Kindle:** fly through a paper lamp with your flame lit — kindle them all to open the gate.
- **Survive:** catch embers to feed your flame; dodge firework rings and black kites.
- **Between nights:** click a card or press 1 / 2 / 3 to take a blessing.
- **P** pause · **R** restart · **M** mute.
